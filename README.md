The game is inspired by “Tregime të moçme shqiptare” (Old Albanian Tales) by Mitrush Kuteli, and it narrates the legendary journey of the Albanian hero, Gjergj Elez Alia, focusing on his endurance of nine wounds and the battles he must face. Players can complete the game in about 1 hour, experiencing a turn-based RPG system with a unique progression based on experience points earned from training and combat.

The game consists of:

1 training scene where the player can hone their skills.
8 battle scenes, each of which must be unlocked in sequence.
Each enemy has unique powers and behaviors, and players must strategize and adapt to defeat them.

Game Features:

Turn-based combat: Players take turns making moves in battle, with decisions affecting the outcome.
Experience and level-up system: Gain experience points from both training and battles to level up and upgrade your hero's abilities.
Unique enemies: Each enemy in the game has specific strengths, weaknesses, and behaviors, providing a variety of challenges.
Technologies Used:

Frontend: Developed with Blazor WebAssembly, using C# for the game logic and Tailwind CSS for styling.
Character Design: Characters are created using HeroForge, providing detailed and customizable 3D models.
Image Assets: Game images are generated using Flux, ensuring a unique visual experience.
Icons: Icons used throughout the game are sourced from Flaticon.
Audio: Sound effects and background music are sourced from Freesound, adding depth to the gameplay experience.
Backend:

Built using ASP.NET Core with a Minimal API.
SQLite is used as the database, with Entity Framework Core managing data persistence for game progress and player stats.
You can experience the game at the following link: game.momotech.al

Nje loje. 
Frymezuar nga “Tregime të moçme shqiptare” nga Mitrush Kuteli.

Loja tregon "rrugetimin" e kreshnikut tone, Gjergj Elez Alisë, ne marrjen e 9 plageve te tij, dhe mund ta mbarosh per rreth 1 ore.

Ka nje skene trajnimi dhe 8 skena beteje, te cilat duhet t’i zhbllokosh sipas renditjes.
Cdo armik ka sjellje dhe "fuqi" unike, dhe do te duhet te pershtatesh per t'i mundur.

Loja eshte ndertuar duke perdorur teknologjite:

Frontend-i eshte zhvilluar ne Blazor WebAssembly, C#, dhe Tailwind CSS.

Karakteret jane krijuar ne HeroForge, imazhet jane gjeneruar nga Flux, ikonat nga Flaticon, dhe audion nga Freesound.

Backend-i eshte ndertuar ne AspNetCore per nje Minimal API, SQLite dhe Entity Framework Core.
